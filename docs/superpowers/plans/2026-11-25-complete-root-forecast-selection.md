## Limits

This isolates adding long forecast outcomes within one fixed broader domain;
it does not isolate breadth against the old three-plan protocol or Pydantic AI
against hand-written parsing. Identical short/long choices would not establish
that horizon is universally irrelevant. Different choices on one state would
not establish general capability or improved whole-campaign strategy. Endpoint
delivery, input sensitivity and goal progress remain separate gates.
